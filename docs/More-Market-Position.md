# Website Content Draft

## Mission
**To empower candidates to prove their growth, protect their identity, and earn recruiter trust in the era of AI-driven competition.**

## Vision
**A recruitment ecosystem where progress speaks louder than résumés, and trust is built through transparency, proof, and fairness.**

## Strategy
- **Protect Candidates:** Enforce anonymity with system-generated aliases until companies transact.  
- **Prove Growth:** Showcase verified milestones, achievements, and proof artifacts in a dynamic Living CV.  
- **Monetize Fairly:** Use the reveal workflow as the monetization gate—subscription for predictable access, contingency for one-off hires.  
- **Build Trust:** Log every reveal and recruiter interaction for transparency and compliance.  

## Differentiators
- **System-Generated Alias:** Candidates cannot expose their real names; identity is revealed only through platform workflows.  
- **Living CV:** A dynamic, infographic-style profile that tracks goals, milestones, skills, certifications, and references.  
- **Hybrid Monetization:** Flexible pricing for companies—subscription discounts or full contingency fees.  
- **Audit Logging:** Every reveal and recruiter interaction is tracked for accountability.  
- **AI-Era Proof:** In a world of generic AI résumés, candidates stand out with verified growth signals.  

## Value Proposition
- **For Candidates:** *“Your Living CV is your proof of progress—protected, verified, and trusted.”*  
- **For Companies:** *“Access top performers through a secure, transparent platform—flexible pricing, guaranteed trust.”*  
- **For Recruiters:** *“Work with a system that enforces fairness, prevents bypassing, and builds long-term credibility.”*  

## Tagline
**“Build credibility, one goal at a time.”**
